Below is a **visual catalog of 20 major edible fig cultivars**. The colors describe **fully ripe fruit under typical growing conditions**; sunlight, climate, irrigation, crop (breba vs. main crop), and degree of ripeness can noticeably alter skin and flesh color. UC research confirms that fig cultivars span skin colors from green/yellow through brown, violet and nearly black, with flesh ranging from amber to strawberry-red. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][1])

### 1. Black Mission

**Skin:** Deep purple → almost black
**Flesh:** Pink to strawberry-red
**Taste:** Rich, intensely sweet, classic fig flavor; notes of berry, honey and dried fruit. Excellent fresh or dried. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][2])

**Flavor family:** 🍓 Berry / fig / honey
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

---

### 2. Brown Turkey

**Skin:** Brown-purple, sometimes greenish near the stem
**Flesh:** Pink to red
**Taste:** Mildly sweet and juicy; less concentrated than Mission, with light honey and fruity notes. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][3])

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Mild honey / fruit
**Sweetness:** ★★★☆☆

---

### 3. Kadota

**Skin:** Light green to yellow-green
**Flesh:** Amber to tan
**Taste:** Mild, clean sweetness with honey notes; generally less berry-like than dark figs. Particularly good for preserves and drying. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][2])

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Honey / sugar
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

---

### 4. Celeste

**Skin:** Bronze-brown to violet-brown
**Flesh:** Rosy amber/pink
**Taste:** Very sweet, smooth and concentrated, with honey and light berry/caramel character. UC describes Celeste as an excellent-flavored fig. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][4])

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Honey / caramel
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

---

### 5. Adriatic

**Skin:** Bright green to yellow-green
**Flesh:** Striking strawberry to dark red
**Taste:** Intensely sweet, fruity and jam-like, often resembling strawberry or raspberry jam.

**Flavor family:** 🍓 Strawberry / berry jam
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

This green-outside/red-inside combination makes Adriatic-type figs among the easiest cultivars to recognize visually.

---

### 6. Desert King

**Skin:** Green to yellow-green
**Flesh:** Strawberry-red
**Taste:** Sweet, juicy and fruity with noticeable berry/strawberry character; less heavy than many dark figs. UC specifically describes Desert King as green-skinned and red-fleshed. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][4])

**Flavor family:** 🍓 Berry / refreshing sweetness
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

---

### 7. Violette de Bordeaux

**Skin:** Dark violet-purple → nearly black
**Flesh:** Deep strawberry-red
**Taste:** Exceptionally rich and concentrated; raspberry/strawberry jam, dark berries and a slight balancing acidity. USDA researchers have compared its flavor to exceptionally good raspberry jam. ([Agricultural Research Magazine][5])

**Flavor family:** 🍇🍓 Dark berry / raspberry jam
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★
**Complexity:** ★★★★★

One of the strongest choices if **flavor quality** is your main criterion.

---

### 8. Calimyrna

**Skin:** Pale green → yellow/golden when ripe
**Flesh:** Amber to pinkish-red
**Taste:** Sweet, nutty and honeyed, often with caramel and dried-fruit notes. Its crunchy fertile seeds contribute significantly to its characteristic texture. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][6])

**Flavor family:** 🌰🍯 Nut / honey / caramel
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

Unlike most cultivars here, Calimyrna is a **Smyrna-type fig and requires pollination (caprification)** for the crop to mature properly. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][7])

---

### 9. Panachée / Tiger Fig

**Skin:** Yellow with dramatic longitudinal green stripes
**Flesh:** Bright strawberry-red
**Taste:** Sweet, fruity and noticeably berry-like, often with strawberry, citrus and honey tones. USDA describes the flesh as having a delicate strawberry-like flavor. ([Agricultural Research Magazine][5])

**Flavor family:** 🍓🍋 Strawberry / citrus
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

Probably the most visually distinctive fig in this catalog.

---

### 10. Conadria

**Skin:** Light green to greenish-yellow
**Flesh:** Pink to light strawberry
**Taste:** Mild-to-moderately sweet, refreshing and fruity; honeyed without being overwhelmingly sugary. Dave Wilson Nursery describes it as somewhat less sweet than Kadota.

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Light honey / fruit
**Sweetness:** ★★★☆☆

---

### 11. Black Madeira

**Skin:** Very dark purple/black, sometimes green around the neck
**Flesh:** Deep crimson to ruby-red
**Taste:** Extremely rich and concentrated—dark berry jam, raspberry, sweetness and a subtle balancing acidity.

**Flavor family:** 🍇🍓 Dark berry / jam
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★
**Complexity:** ★★★★★

Black Madeira is frequently regarded by fig collectors as a benchmark **premium berry-flavored fig**.

---

### 12. Hardy Chicago / Chicago Hardy

**Skin:** Brown-purple to dark violet; sometimes greenish-brown
**Flesh:** Strawberry-red
**Taste:** Sweet and jammy with strawberry/berry characteristics; stronger flavor than many ordinary brown figs.

**Flavor family:** 🍓 Strawberry jam / fig
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

Its major horticultural advantage is its unusually good cold tolerance compared with many other *Ficus carica* cultivars. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][8])

---

### 13. LSU Purple

**Skin:** Purple to dark purple
**Flesh:** Pink to strawberry-red
**Taste:** Sweet, rich and smooth, with berry-like and somewhat complex fig flavors. Fig Database classifies its flavor in the rich/exotic group. ([figdatabase.com][9])

**Flavor family:** 🍇 Berry / rich fig
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

---

### 14. White Genoa

**Skin:** Pale green to yellow-green
**Flesh:** Amber, pale pink to reddish-pink
**Taste:** Mild, sweet and honeyed, sometimes with a subtle lemony character. ([figgipedia.com][10])

**Flavor family:** 🍯🍋 Honey / light citrus
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

This is a much lighter flavor profile than Black Madeira or Violette de Bordeaux.

---

### 15. Brunswick

**Skin:** Green-brown to bronze/purple-brown
**Flesh:** Amber-pink to reddish
**Taste:** Sweet, soft and juicy with a traditional fig/honey flavor; generally less intensely berry-like than the premium dark cultivars.

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Classic fig / honey
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

---

### 16. Excel

**Skin:** Green to yellow-green
**Flesh:** Amber/golden
**Taste:** Richly sweet but relatively clean and mild, dominated by sugar and honey rather than berries.

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Honey / sugar
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

The photograph shows the characteristic amber interior especially clearly.

---

### 17. Olympian

**Skin:** Bronze-brown to reddish-purple
**Flesh:** Amber to reddish-pink
**Taste:** Sweet, juicy and honeyed, with light berry/fruity notes rather than an extremely concentrated jam flavor.

**Flavor family:** 🍯🍓 Honey / light berry
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

Olympian is notable for producing particularly large fruit.

---

### 18. Peter's Honey

**Skin:** Yellow-green to pale green
**Flesh:** Amber/golden
**Taste:** Pronounced honey sweetness—sweet, syrupy and relatively low in berry acidity.

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Honey
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

This is a good example of the **“honey fig” flavor class**: the attraction is sugary/honey richness rather than strawberry or raspberry flavor.

---

### 19. Italian Honey / Lattarula

**Skin:** Green → yellow-green when fully ripe
**Flesh:** Amber to light brown/pink
**Taste:** Very sweet, syrupy and distinctly honey-like; mild fruitiness with little acidity.

**Flavor family:** 🍯 Honey / syrup
**Sweetness:** ★★★★★

Lattarula is therefore quite different from an Adriatic or Black Madeira even though all are very sweet—the sweetness is **honey-like rather than berry-jam-like**.

---

### 20. Black Jack

**Skin:** Purplish-brown to very dark purple
**Flesh:** Pink to strawberry-red
**Taste:** Sweet and juicy with a classic fig/strawberry character; broadly similar to Black Mission but generally considered somewhat less intense. UC describes Black Jack as having purple skin and sweet pink flesh. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][4])

**Flavor family:** 🍓 Fig / strawberry
**Sweetness:** ★★★★☆

---

A useful way to think about these 20 is that they fall into roughly **four flavor families**: **berry/jam figs** such as Black Madeira, Violette de Bordeaux and Adriatic; **honey figs** such as Peter's Honey and Lattarula; **classic balanced figs** such as Mission, Celeste and Black Jack; and **nutty/caramel figs**, particularly Calimyrna. Sensory research also shows that cultivar flavor can include berry, honey, melon, stone-fruit, citrus and other components rather than simply varying in sweetness. ([UC Agriculture and Natural Resources][3])

**For pure eating quality**, a strong five-variety tasting set would be **Black Madeira → Violette de Bordeaux → Adriatic → Panachée → Calimyrna**. They give you five quite different expressions of what a fig can taste like rather than five cultivars that are merely variations of the same flavor.
